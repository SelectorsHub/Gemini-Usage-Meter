While a Gemini tab is open. With no Gemini tab open it checks at most every 5 minutes. The meter also updates instantly whenever Gemini refreshes its own usage, and right after each reply.

How it works

The meter reads the same usage figures Gemini shows in Settings › Usage limits, using your signed-in Gemini session in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and no data leaves your browser. Chat size is an estimate counted from the conversation on the page.
